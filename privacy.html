Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Who we are

Resenha Cortes runs a private tool that publishes our own video clips to our own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

2. Data we access

When the account owner authorizes the tool, the platform gives us an access token and basic account information (such as account ID and display name). We use them only to upload videos and read the status of our own posts.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or other users. We do not use cookies or tracking on this site.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on our private server, are never shared with third parties, and can be revoked at any time in the platform's settings.

5. Deletion

Revoking the app's access in TikTok, Google or Meta settings removes our access. We delete stored tokens when they are revoked or no longer needed.
